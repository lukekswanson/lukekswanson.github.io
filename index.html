MOST WHO DID NEVER SENT A PAYMENT. Research made the cause clear: merchants didn't know what QuickBooks Payments could actually do for them. Setup screens were written around compliance steps and feature options — not outcomes. Without a concrete picture of what they'd gain, merchants couldn't justify finishing. And without finishing, they never found out. My hypothesis: fix the content, fix the activation problem. If merchants understood the value before they were asked to commit, more would complete setup — and first use would follow. RESEARCH MERCHANTS DIDN'T KNOW WHAT THEY WERE SIGNING UP FOR. Usability sessions and unmoderated tests revealed a consistent pattern: merchants weren't confused by the steps — they were uncertain about the destination. 6 of 7 participants called Payments their most important feature. Most still couldn't complete setup without help. A full content audit mapped every screen against what merchants actually needed at that moment. Most were answering questions nobody had asked — and missing the one question everyone had: what will I be able to do when this is done? "I don't know which one to pick. What if I pick the wrong one and can't change it?" — Merchant, usability session ITERATIONS LEAD WITH THE OUTCOME. EARN EVERY WORD AFTER THAT. I reframed every setup screen around what merchants would be able to do — not what they needed to provide. I tested multiple content approaches for payment method selection, iterating from feature-led to use-case-led to customer-experience-led framing. For the AI assistant, early versions over-explained. Each iteration cut further — one suggestion, one action, done. ￼ FTU setup checklist — "choose how to get paid" ￼ Payments agent — congrats on first payment request ￼ Intuit Assist — empty state before customer selected ￼ Intuit Assist — populated with customer context SOLUTION CONTENT MAPPED TO EVERY STAGE OF THE MERCHANT JOURNEY. The final system gave each lifecycle stage a clear content goal and a matched AI message — from first setup through ongoing use. The AI invoice assistant got a full content framework: proactive suggestions grounded in merchant data, adjustable-tone reminders, AI-driven late fee recommendations, and a "good to go" state that confirmed completion without adding noise. ￼ Review and edit invoice reminders — tone selector ￼ Scheduled reminders — preview with merchant controls ￼ Late fee suggestion — AI recommendation with merchant data ￼ Late fees applied — "Good to go" IMPACT SETUP COMPLETION JUMPED FROM UNDER 30% TO OVER 60%. <30% → 60%+ Payments setup completion rate — before and after 4× Growth in monthly payment volume during this period (Intuit FY2024) 21% YoY growth in online services revenue driven by Payments (Intuit FY2024) I CARE ABOUT THE DETAILS.
